# SOURCES & ALTERATIONS — Batch A (Foundation, Scenarios 5–11)

*How these scenarios were made, what they rest on, and what was deliberately kept out. Companion to `sources-and-alterations.md` (Scenarios 1–4).*

**Method for this batch:** Foundation scenarios teach single procedural skills. Each is **process-generic from the verified law library — no single case source**: the spine is a recurring, publicly documented system pattern (a referral mailed to a stale address; a summary that simplifies a careful sentence; a voluntary plan with no end date), written as a fictional composite family. No published opinion is dramatized, no transcript is quoted, and — per the standing doctrine — no private case from the user’s orbit or files was used or consulted. Names, children, workers, addresses, ID numbers (the 4185/4815 pair in Scenario 10 is invented), dates, and workplaces are fictional and were altered/created for this batch; any resemblance to a specific family is the resemblance of common system paperwork, not of a person.

**Law used (shared verified set only):** every scenario’s debriefs and strategy guides rest on 55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d) (record release on request) as the load-bearing right; Scenario 11 additionally references the counsel right (42 Pa.C.S. § 6337) in its guide only, as a documented practice pillar. Deadlines that drive play (Scenario 5’s appointment time, Scenario 6’s slot date, Scenario 7’s response date, Scenario 10’s list review) are agency/school/program dates stated inside the fiction — not statutory clocks, and the texts say so where it matters. No other statute is cited anywhere in this batch; in particular Scenario 11 invents **no** interpretation entitlement and states that discipline in its own debrief.

**Flags:** `recordsRequested` is set in all seven scenarios (each contains a records/copy beat). The `removed` flag appears **nowhere** in this batch: in Scenarios 5, 6, 7, 10, and 11 the children are home throughout; in Scenario 9 the child stays with a neighbor under a *voluntary* safety plan inside the story — a consent arrangement, not a removal, and the engine’s 72-hour banner would be false there; in Scenario 8 the child was placed *before* the story opens, so no removal occurs on stage either.

**Dates:** each scenario uses one coherent 2026 start date; every kicker date was generated from (start date + dayOffset) and machine-checked, and every weekday named in body text was checked against the 2026 calendar during generation review.
