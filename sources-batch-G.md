## Newly verified law (primary sources, this run)

**42 Pa.C.S. § 6336.1 (Notice and hearing), as amended by Act 48 of 2023, and the definition at 42 Pa.C.S. § 6302.** Read in full from the text of Act 48 of 2023 (HB 1058, approved December 14, 2023; effective in 60 days), which adds "potential kinship care resource" to § 6336.1(a) and adds the § 6302 definition: *"An individual who meets the requirements to be an emergency caregiver and has expressed a willingness to be a kinship caregiver for a dependent child."* As amended, § 6336.1(a) provides: timely notice of hearings to the foster parent, preadoptive parent, relative providing care, **or a potential kinship care resource under 67 Pa.C.S. § 7507(c)**; the right to be heard at any hearing for the foster parent, preadoptive parent, or relative providing care; for a potential kinship care resource, the right to be heard at a scheduled hearing or a separate dispositional hearing **only as to the individual's qualifications to provide kinship care**, after which the court **shall render a decision** whether the potential kinship care resource may receive notice or participate in future hearings; and **no legal standing** for any of them absent legal custody under § 6357. Scenarios 49–56 use this law as worded here; Scenarios 47–48 use only its long-standing foster-parent notice/hearing core. (The pre-2023 core is corroborated by the v1 research base, incl. In re D.K., 922 A.2d 929 (Pa. Super. 2007).)

No other new law was admitted. Kinship mechanics beyond the v1 verified set (study contents, courtesy studies between counties, reconsideration practice) are presented in the scenarios as *documented process*, never as cited statute.

## Published-record sources and documented patterns, by scenario

- **S47 "The First Night"** — Emergency nighttime removal with kin named at the door; shelter-care placement with kin. Pattern spine from In re K.T.W.E., No. 333 WDA 2020 (Pa. Super. 2020) (project-documented in v1: a relative unavailable at the night call shapes the whole case), run in the direction v1's S4 could not take: the relative named *in writing*, with a number, at the door.
- **S48 "The Spare Room"** — Kinship home study as a finite, climbable checklist (clearances, medical statement, home fixes made before the visit). Documented study practice; no single opinion spine.
- **S49 "Two Houses"** — Sibling set split for bed count after a housing emergency; household-member disclosure as the study's hinge. Sibling-bond textures corroborated at summary level by published sibling-placement appeals (e.g., In re J.E.L.-B. & J.C.-B., Pa. Super. 2016 — read at search-summary level only; used as texture, not law).
- **S50 "The Move Nobody Announced"** — Placement move decided by internal memo weeks before the parent is told; continuity/bonding weight. Texture from In re T.S.M., 71 A.3d 251 (Pa. 2013) (multiple placements; the child's timeline), read this run at opinion-excerpt level; no § 2511 substance imported — the scenario is a placement review, not a termination case.
- **S51 "The Second Study"** — Kinship denial resting on factual error (merged household; an uncounted finished room); correction by documents + the party's record right; reconsideration rather than any invented appeal. Posture consistent with the v1-documented line that agency approval processes and court placement authority are distinct (In re D.S., 979 A.2d 901 (Pa. Super. 2009); In re J.P., Pa. Super. 2010).
- **S52 "The Notice on the Fridge"** — Foster-parent thirty-day disruption notice; a teenager defaulted to congregate care; family finding that stopped at one misdialed call. Documented patterns (foster disruption notices; single-attempt family finding; teen placement scarcity) — composites, no single opinion spine.
- **S53 "Every Bed in the County"** — ASFA 15-of-22 approaching with siblings split and a relative whose "no" was to a different question than the file recorded. ASFA framework per the v1 verified set (42 U.S.C. § 675(5)(E) and its three exceptions, as taught in v1's S3 from In re J.L.L. et al.).
- **S54 "The Assessment Nobody Signed"** — A placement recommendation resting on one mis-scored assessment item, contradicted by the visitation center's primary logs. Assessment-disclosure machinery per the v1 verified set: 55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d)/(f) and Pa.R.J.C.P. 1340(A), (E), (F), with the Universal-Assessment application of Rule 1340 flagged **UNTESTED** in the data, as in v1 (S24/S31/S33). The item-level error is a documented-pattern composite (scoring from summaries of summaries).
- **S55 "The County Line"** — Inter-county move after a foster-home emergency; kin identified at intake whose "mailed packet" never arrived and whose own calls sit in the agency's notes; courtesy study lost between counties. Documented patterns; the February/October dates are the scenario's evidence, built to be audited.
- **S56 "The Wrong Gregory"** — Two parallel paper failures: the mother's own indicated report (appeal under Act 119's burden-shifting, per the v1 verified set from the Act 119 primary text) and a kinship study stalled by a same-name identity conflation in a clearance summary (name-only database matching — documented pattern). No real person's report, name, or record is depicted; both Gregorys are inventions, and the conflation mechanism is described generically.
