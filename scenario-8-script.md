**ENDING.** Debrief:

- Nothing in this ending is a ruling, and it does not pretend to be one: Wren is still placed, and the road home runs through hearings this scenario does not depict. What the visits built is a record — arrival times, a kept drawing, a book resumed at the right page, goodbyes on time, and a parent's notes that agree with the supervisor's in every particular.
- The law inside this story: under 55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d), the visit records were released on request. Reading your own record is how you learn what the next reader will believe about you — while there is still time to add your half of it.
- Notice what the strategy actually was: prepare for the hour like it matters (it is the whole case, twice a week), and write the day down the day it happens. Love supplied the visit. Paper supplied the evidence of it.

Judges weigh records through their own experience, and luck decides which pen you get. What no pen can take from you is your own dated page, kept at home, that says what the day actually held.
