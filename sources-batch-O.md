# Sources and Alterations — Batch O (Wave 2, Batch 10: Capstones, Scenarios 127–136)

**What this batch is built from — stated plainly.** This batch read **no new published opinions** and verified **no new law**. Every scenario is a composite built from (a) the verified law set below, and (b) recurring documented practice patterns already recorded in this project's earlier sources files (notably `sources-and-alterations.md` and Wave 1's batch files): multi-clock sibling cases, cross-county kinship studies, vendor waitlists outrunning service plans, indicated appeals running beside dependency cases, and permanency hearings decided by the completeness of a year-long record. No real person's case — and none of the private cases in the project owner's orbit — is depicted or drawn on. All families, workers, lawyers, judges, and documents are fictional composites.

(f)(9), and (f.1)); kinship procedures and definitions (42 Pa.C.S. § 6336.1, Act 48 of 2023; § 6302); ASFA's filing duty at 15 of the most recent 22 months and its three exceptions (42 U.S.C. § 675(5)(E)); release of the case record on request (55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d)) and its redaction rule (§ 3130.44(f)); Pa.R.J.C.P. 1340(A),(B)(1),(E),(F) with the Universal Assessment application flagged UNTESTED (no scenario in this batch turns on it, and no assessment is described anywhere in this batch as AI, machine learning, or predictive).

**Alterations and deviations, disclosed:**
- S127 carries **3 endings** against the Foundation band's 2: the capstone's two clocks (appeal window and review) have independent failure modes, and collapsing them would have taught that a missed appeal is recoverable inside the case. It is not, so the registry has its own ending.
- S128 carries **4 endings** against the Hard band's 3: the upheld-appeal ending exists to teach 23 Pa.C.S. § 6341(g)'s post-decision clocks (reconsideration within 15 days of the mailing date; Commonwealth Court appeal within 30 days), which no other ending in the batch reaches.
- S129's packet node is titled “The Packet, Assembled” and its month-nine beat is the node titled “Month Nine Review” — titles as built; the strategy guide's route section uses the built titles verbatim.
- S132's playable story opens at month thirteen-plus (offsets are counted from the entry date, so its first node carries the entry as narrated backstory and its hearing lands Monday, October 12, 2026, ahead of the Wednesday, November 4, 2026 fifteen-month date).
- Two S136 node ids preserve an authoring swap (`study_travels` holds the study-completion beat; `kin_ready` holds the Bureau hearing beat). Content, order, dates, and gates are as designed; ids are internal.
- Character names in this batch were re-checked against every prior scenario file in the folder at build time; colliding names found in Wave 2 batches 6–9's usage (and two surnames found in the earlier corpus) were renamed inside this batch before delivery. The renames are Basque surnames/given names chosen for zero corpus hits; no renamed character corresponds to any real person.
- Wins labeled **partial** (S130, S131, S135, S136) are labeled in their strategy guides and ending debriefs: the children spend substantial time in care on every route, and the guides say so. Wins labeled **full** (S127–129, S132–134) return the child(ren) and close the track(s) completely on the walked route.
