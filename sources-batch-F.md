# Sources and Alterations — Batch F: Scenarios 37–46, "The Front Door"

*Wave 1 expansion, under the owner's doctrine: scenarios built from "likely or documented situations that could happen and how to win the best possible outcome." Documented = published PA Superior/Commonwealth Court opinions and documented system patterns; likely = composites of recurring documented practice. Every family below is a fictional composite. No real person's identifiable case is used; the private cases in the owner's orbit (Christine May, Annie Roth, Camden Ransing) were excluded as sources in full. Drafts only — nothing sent or published; attorney verification remains the gate before any organizational or parent-facing use, as with all Tactic Watch material.*

## Provenance disclosure (read first)

**This batch's source map is reconstructed, not contemporaneous.** The original Batch 1 team generated the ten scenario JSONs but ended before filing its sources file, its scripts, or its strategy guides, and filed no report. The completion pass (this file, the ten scripts, and the ten strategy guides) was produced afterward from the shipped scenario data itself. What follows is therefore an honest reconstruction: where a scenario's text itself identifies the documented pattern it is built on — a statutory mechanism quoted in its own debriefs, a procedural posture that matches a documented appellate pattern — that basis is stated. Where the specific opinion or pattern source cannot be identified from the scenario's content, the scenario is labeled what it demonstrably is: a **documented-pattern composite**, built from recurring, publicly documented features of Pennsylvania CPS investigation practice, with no specific source claimed. **No citation in this file was invented to fill the gap.** If attorney verification requires contemporaneous sourcing for any individual scenario, that scenario should be re-sourced or held — the reconstruction is a floor, not a substitute.

## Engine-flag note (removed / recordsRequested)

`recordsRequested` is set on at least one choice on the winning route of all ten scenarios (walker-enforced). `removed` required two completion-pass corrections, both because Batch I's discipline — the flag is set where, and only where, a removal physically occurs inside the playable story — was not followed in the generated data:

- **Scenario 45**: the children are removed on night one, on the story's main spine, and the flag was set nowhere. It is now set on both choices leaving the start node (both converge on the first night), so the 72-hour shelter-care banner fires on every path.
- **Scenario 41**: the voluntary-placement branch at Marla's is, in the scenario's own words, "not a removal," and correctly carries no flag. The declined branch is an emergency placement ordered by a judge, with a shelter care hearing "now required by law" — a removal in fact. The flag is now set on the declining choice only, so the banner fires on exactly the branch where the clock runs.
- Scenarios 37–40, 42–44, and 46 depict investigations, determinations, and appeals without any in-story removal; the flag is absent by design.

## Per-scenario map (reconstructed from content)

- **S37 "The Bruise at Practice" (Foundation, 18 nodes, 2 endings)** — documented-pattern composite. A sports bruise reported after the fact, answered by a pediatric chart dated before the report. Law in data: § 3130.44(d).
- **S38 "The Card in the Door" (Foundation, 18 nodes, 2 endings)** — documented-pattern composite. The business-card-in-the-door opening and the first-call summary are documented fixtures of investigation practice; the scenario's subject — the written confirmation of a phone call as the file's first draft — is a practice pattern, not a sourced case. Law in data: § 3130.44(d).
- **S39 "The Interview at School" (Foundation, 18 nodes, 2 endings)** — documented-pattern composite. School interviews of children before parental notice are a documented, recurring feature of PA investigations; the scenario dramatizes no interview and rests on the record that follows it. No specific source identifiable; none claimed. Law in data: § 3130.44(d).
- **S40 "The Letter with a Case Number" (Foundation, 18 nodes, 2 endings)** — documented-pattern composite. Investigation-by-letter with a response window; the envelope/postmark beat is evidence-handling practice. No specific source identifiable; none claimed. Law in data: § 3130.44(d).
- **S41 "The Night They Came Twice" (Hard, 24 nodes, 3 endings)** — documented-pattern composite with a statutory spine. Two versions of one oral safety agreement, a voluntary placement agreement offered at a kitchen table, and — on the declined branch — an emergency placement with a shelter care hearing inside 72 hours (§ 6332; § 6315(d), stated in the data). The voluntary-vs-ordered placement distinction is documented practice; no specific opinion is identifiable from the text. Law in data: § 6332/§ 6315(d), § 3130.44(d).
- **S42 "The Report from the Hospital" (Hard, 24 nodes, 3 endings)** — documented-pattern composite with a statutory spine. Conflicting medical readings of a toddler's fracture and an indicated determination answered first at the determination meeting and, on lesser branches, through the full Act 119 appeal path ((a)(2), (c.2)(4), (c.2)(5), (c.3), (g) — all stated in the data with correct windows). Conflicting-consult fracture cases are a documented appellate genre in PA; the scenario names none, and this file claims none. Law in data: § 6341 family, § 3130.44(d).
- **S43 "Two Reports, One Week" (Hard, 24 nodes, 3 endings)** — documented-pattern composite. Parallel investigations merging into one narrative is a recurring documented failure mode of multi-report cases; the separation discipline is practice, stated without citation in the data. Law in data: § 3130.44(d).
- **S44 "The Morning Test" (Hard, 24 nodes, 3 endings)** — documented-pattern composite. A prescription-positive screen and the sequencing of the prescriber's letter; the data states no drug-testing rule and invents none — its only citation is § 3130.44(d), for reading the file's claim before answering it. - **S45 "The First Thirty Days" (Complex, 36 nodes, 4 endings)** — documented-pattern composite with the batch's fullest statutory spine: removal, the 72-hour shelter care hearing (§ 6332; § 6315(d)), counsel (§ 6337; § 6332(a)), kinship vs. foster arms, a scored assessment requested under § 3130.44(d), redaction pressed under § 3130.44(f), and an item-by-item rebuttal framed under Pa.R.J.C.P. 1340(A), (B)(1), (E), (F) with the assessment application expressly flagged untested in the data. - **S46 "Two Workers, One File" (Complex, 36 nodes, 4 endings)** — documented-pattern composite with a statutory spine.
