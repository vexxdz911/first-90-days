The First 90 Days — Scenario Library

Thirty-six role-playing scenarios of what the child welfare system puts a parent through — built from the published court record, composited so no real family is recognizable. Version 1.

Fictional composite stories — no real family's case · Prototype — pending attorney verification · General information, not legal advice. Content warning: these stories involve investigations, court hearings, and children being removed. Every scenario has a Quick Exit, and every comic edition has a Watch Mode — it plays itself with random choices, so a room can watch without anyone playing.
