# THE FIRST 90 DAYS — Scenario 11: “The Interpreter” (Foundation tier)

**The family is fictional — a composite. No real person's story appears here.**

**Content note:** This scenario depicts a planning meeting conducted in a parent’s second language, English-only forms, and a written request for interpretation. There is no graphic content. Players may skip any scene or use the quick exit at any time.

**How to read effects:** `cash −4` changes the player's cash. `FLAG: name` sets a flag the engine tracks. `HIDDEN + item` means the case file quietly records that item at this choice — the player does not see it when it happens. `NOTE:` is text shown to the player right after choosing.

## [n2] The Meeting Before the Meeting

*Day 14 — Monday, October 19, 2026 (dayOffset 14)*

**ENDING.** Debrief:

- The win is understanding, made durable: a meeting held with interpretation, papers read at full speed, choices — including a declined one — recorded as choices, and a signature that means what signatures are supposed to mean. - The law inside this story: under 55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d), the copies of the record are released on request — the request that produced your copy of the packet is the same motion, in the same writing, as the one that asked for the interpreter and the translation. Paper asks travel; spoken asks evaporate.
