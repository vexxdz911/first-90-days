# Strategy Guide — Scenario 50: The Move Nobody Announced

**The win, named exactly as in the scenario data: "Ending — Same School, Family Address."**

The best outcome: at the May 21st review, the court stops the cross-county move and orders Ruben placed with his cousin Anita — two streets from his school — with expedited kinship approval to be completed within thirty days and the foster mother hosting the transition.

Final cash on the walked route: $153 of $165. The outcome gate requires `packetBuilt`, `fosterTimeline`, and `kinWritten` — the assembled hearing packet, the foster mother's March 30th notice date documented, and Anita's kinship request in writing with clearances moving.

**Verified law used:** the record request (55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d)) — the engine of the scenario: the agency's April 2nd move memo, its "Parent to be notified at next scheduled contact" line, and the supervisor emails showing the notify-task reassigned twice all come from the record, not the summary; the foster parent's notice and right to be heard, and the potential kinship care resource's right to be heard on qualifications (42 Pa.C.S. § 6336.1, as amended by Act 48 of 2023; definition at § 6302).

**Traps traced to endings:**

- **The hallway compromise** (`agency_meeting`): the agency offers the school year with the foster mother and a July move — everything except the kinship piece. Accepting a compromise in a hallway spends the hearing; the winning route declines *in writing, with thanks and reasons*, and lets the court decide. (The decline is flagged in the data; the hearing is where the packet outperforms the offer.)
- **"Ending — A Softer Landing (partial)":** the move softened by the filed paper — school year finished, transition ordered, Anita approved for weekends with her study kept open and dated. The guide preserves the v1 doctrine verbatim: a courtroom loss with a strong record outperforms a courtroom absence with a strong grievance.
- **"Ending — The Bus Route Changed":** the unfiled objection. Nothing was requested, nothing was read, the memo's plan executed on schedule. Its debrief is the scenario's thesis: placement moves are administrative until a party makes them judicial — in writing, fast.

**What no strategy controls:** the receiving home's quality (here genuinely good — the scenario refuses the cheap villain), the judge's appetite for mid-year stability arguments, and Anita herself: a kinship resource who falters in her own study ends this differently no matter how good the packet is. The packet's job was to force the comparison — the move against the child's *documented* life, instead of against nothing.
