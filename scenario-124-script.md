## [end_whole_page] Ending — The Whole Page, Read Aloud

*Day 52 — Thursday, December 10, 2026*

**Ending.**

**Debrief:** The findings come in the statute's order, and each one cites the record that carried it. Compliance: found — the arithmetic table, undisputed. Progress toward alleviating the circumstances of placement (42 Pa.C.S. § 6351(f)(3)): found — on the treatment arc read entire, the provider's full context, and the attachment record of eleven logged visits. On efforts, the court's sentence is dry and permanent: the assessment item relied upon had been flagged by its own rater for re-observation, and was carried into a court report unre-observed. The order is a stepped return: overnights beginning in January, Callum home by spring if the steps hold, therapy continuing through the transition on Dr. Sayegh's stated plan.

Name the doctrine precisely, because this ending is its cleanest statement in the library. Nothing was falsified; everything was *excerpted* — and excerpting is defeated not by indignation but by completeness, filed in advance, in the statute's order: the complete record requested (55 Pa. Code § 3130.44(d)); the author restored to her page; reports examined and controverted as the disposition principle contemplates (42 Pa.C.S. § 6341(d)(2)); a single-visit score answered by its population of visits, through a discovery rule whose reach to that score was flagged, in writing, as untested. (Pa.R.J.C.P. 1340(A), (E), (F)) The court did not find that the agency lied. It found, from the whole record, that the truth was longer than the quotations.
