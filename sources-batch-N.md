# Sources & Alterations — Batch N (Scenarios 117–126, Education & Medical)

Wave 2, Batch 9 of "The First 90 Days" expansion. Doctrine (the owner's words): scenarios built from "likely or documented situations that could happen" — documented = published PA Superior/Commonwealth Court opinions and documented system patterns; likely = composites of recurring documented practice. Fictional composites ONLY; no real person's identifiable case; the private cases in the owner's orbit are excluded as sources. Every scenario is winnable to its best outcome, with the route graph-walked programmatically (see `walk-117-126.py`; results summarized at the end). All ten scenarios were generated programmatically (`batchN_lib.py` + `gen-117-118.py`, `gen-119-120.py`, `gen-121-122.py`, `gen-123-124.py`, `gen-125-126.py`) so that dates, cash, and flag arithmetic are computed, not typed, and validated by `validate-117-126.py`.

## Newly verified law (verified from primary/official text during this run)

The assignment's hard caution governed: education-law and medical-consent rules are stated as law ONLY where verified from primary sources during this run. Four authorities were so verified; everything else education/medical in these scenarios is framed as orders, practice, forms, and dependency-court mechanics from the pre-verified set.

1. **20 U.S.C. § 6311(g)(1)(E)(i)–(iii)** (ESSA state-plan assurances for children in foster care): the LEA assurances that a child in foster care will enroll or remain in the school of origin **unless a determination is made that it is not in the child's best interest** (§ (g)(1)(E)(i)); that the determination be based on all factors relating to the child's best interest, **including consideration of the appropriateness of the current educational setting and the proximity to the school in which the child is enrolled at the time of placement** (§ (g)(1)(E)(ii)); and that, if it is not in the child's best interest, the child be **immediately enrolled in a new school, even if the child is unable to produce records normally required for enrollment**, with the enrolling school immediately contacting the school last attended to obtain relevant academic and other records (§ (g)(1)(E)(iii)). Basis: the statutory text as quoted verbatim in a 2026 Illinois Appellate Court opinion (Justia) and in a Nebraska Department of Education ESSA excerpt document, cross-checked against each other word-for-word. Used in S117, S121, S126.
2. **20 U.S.C. § 6312(c)(5)(B)**: LEAs receiving Title I funds must collaborate with the State or local child welfare agency to (among other things) designate a point of contact and **develop and implement clear written procedures governing how transportation to maintain children in foster care in their school of origin, when in their best interest, will be provided, arranged, and funded for the duration of the child's time in foster care**; where additional costs are incurred, they are provided if the local child welfare agency reimburses, the LEA pays, or the two agree to share the cost. Basis: ESEA text excerpts (Illinois State Board of Education ESSA excerpt PDF presenting § 6312(c)(5) in full; Justia U.S. Code text of § 6312(c)(5)). Used in S117, S121, S126. Scenarios say "shared cost" and "written procedures" — the statute's own words.
3. **34 C.F.R. § 300.301(b), (c)(1)**: (b) a request for an initial evaluation to determine eligibility may be initiated by a parent of the child or by a public agency; (c)(1) the initial evaluation must be conducted **within 60 days of receiving parental consent** for the evaluation, or **within the timeframe established by the State** if the State establishes a timeframe. Basis: eCFR text via Cornell LII (§ 300.301) and the U.S. Department of Education's IDEA site reg text, cross-checked. Used in S118, S123. The scenarios deliberately state the federal 60-day frame **and** the state-timeframe exception, and assert no Pennsylvania day count, because no PA-established timeframe was verified during this run.
4. **34 C.F.R. § 300.519(a), (d)(2)(i), (g), (h)**: (a) an SEA must ensure the rights of a child are protected when no parent can be identified, the agency cannot locate a parent after reasonable efforts, the child is a ward of the State, or the child is an unaccompanied homeless youth; for a ward of the State, the surrogate parent may alternatively be appointed by the judge overseeing the child's case. (d)(2)(i) (with the surrounding (d) standards quoted in the guide): a person selected as a surrogate must have no personal or professional interest conflicting with the child's interest, and employees of the SEA, LEA, or any other agency involved in the education or care of the child are barred. (g) the SEA must make reasonable efforts to ensure assignment of a surrogate **not more than 30 days** after determining the child needs one. (h) the surrogate may represent the child in all matters relating to identification, evaluation, educational placement, and the provision of FAPE. Basis: eCFR text via Cornell LII (§ 300.519), cross-checked against Wrightslaw's reproduction of the same regulation. Used in S123.

## Deliberate non-use

- **No medical-consent statute is asserted anywhere in this batch.** Who consents to dental surgery (S119) and to medication changes (S120, S126) is litigated entirely through the dependency court's order-writing and review machinery (§ 6351(f)) and through documentation practice. The guides state plainly that a parallel body of parental-consent law likely exists but was not verified from primary sources during this run. A "parents retain rights not expressly limited" proposition is described as general understanding in strategy-119, not cited as law.
- **No IDEA rules beyond §§ 300.301/300.519 are stated**: no eligibility categories, no IEP-content rules, no stay-put (§ 300.518 not used), no manifestation-determination rule (S118's discipline subplot is handled as a dependency finding, not an IDEA holding), no § 300.502 independent-evaluation right.
- **McKinney-Vento is not invoked**: foster-care school stability runs under ESSA §§ 6311/6312 here, not McKinney-Vento's homeless-youth provisions (dependency-placement stability and McKinney-Vento overlap is real-world contested; the batch takes no position).
- **FERPA is not stated as law**: school records move in these scenarios through the school's own correction process (S122), the § 6311(g)(1)(E)(iii) inter-school records contact, and § 3130.44(d) for the agency record — no FERPA provision is quoted or paraphrased as a holding.
- **55 Pa. Code § 2511 was NOT used** (it appeared in an earlier draft plan for S119 and does not exist in the verified set; nothing in the emitted scenarios relies on it).
- **No assessment is called AI/ML/predictive or an algorithm** (validator sweeps for all four terms): S124's instrument is described only as a scored assessment summary with item responses and a configuration sheet, and its Rule 1340 application is flagged UNTESTED in the data, the order the scenario wins, and the guide.

## Scenario sources, patterns, and alterations

- **S117 "The Bus That Crosses the Line" (Foundation).** Pattern: school-of-origin determinations made at placement with transportation treated as a household problem. Source discipline: §§ 6311(g)(1)(E)/6312(c)(5)(B) as newly verified. All names, the district, the mileage, and the route plan are invented composites.
- **S118 "Sixty Days from a Signature" (Hard).** Pattern: parent-initiated evaluation requests stalling between an agency referral that was never transmitted and a district waiting for a consent signature; discipline records filling the evidentiary vacuum at permanency. Law: § 300.301(b), (c)(1) as newly verified. Alteration note: the scenario counts the federal 60 days and names the state-timeframe exception rather than asserting a PA clock.
- **S119 "Two Signatures" (Hard).** Pattern: routine provider visits consented by resource caregivers as a practice, discovered by a parent after the fact; no governing order text beyond a blanket "arrange care" line. No consent statute asserted (see non-use). The win is an allocation order, not a liability finding.
- **S120 "The Medicine Log" (Hard).** Pattern: psychotropic dose changes communicated to parents anecdotally or not at all; foster-home medication logs kept informally. Dependency-side documentation duties only.
- **S121 "The Meeting That Happened Without Her" (Complex).** Pattern: best-interest determinations convened on agency/district schedules that structurally exclude working parents, then treated as settled. The scenario does NOT claim the ex parte determination was void — no such authority was verified — it wins a reconvened, complete determination and a funding order. ASFA date arithmetic: placed August 10, 2026; +15 calendar months = **November 10, 2027** (machine-audited; a Tuesday/Wednesday error in the first draft of the debrief was caught by the date-assertion step in the generator and corrected).
- **S122 "Excused, Absent, Unexcused" (Complex).** Pattern: attendance-data migration/coding errors propagating from school portals into petitions and indicated reports as unverified counts. The win runs on the school's own correction and § 6341(c) inaccuracy, not on any claim that truancy can never ground dependency — nine genuinely unexcused days remain in the proved case, deliberately.
- **S123 "The Empty Chair" (Complex).** Pattern: surrogate-parent appointments resting on thin "cannot locate" documentation (stale addresses, unlogged calls), and conflict-of-interest screening done casually. Law: § 300.519 as newly verified. The interim surrogate's acts are ratified in the win, deliberately — unwinding them would harm the child.
- **S124 "The Whole Page" (Complex).** Continues the v1/Wave 1 assessment-summary arc (S24/S31 lineage) in the medical-records register: excerpted provider language and a redacted summary configuration. Rule 1340 flagged UNTESTED throughout; § 3130.44(d)/(f) carries the record fight.
- **S125 "Page Nine of the Chart" (Complex).** Pattern: indicated findings in infant-fracture investigations where the complete chart contains a specialist consult the summary omits; the CPSL appeal (§ 6341, Act 119) run as the evidentiary main event while the dependency track proceeds separately at kinship. Dates machine-audited: notice April 12, 2027 + 90 days = **Sunday, July 11, 2027** (§ 6341(g) counted as in prior batches); hearing June 9, 2027 → decision June 25, 2027 = 16 days, inside § 6341(c.3)'s 45 days. The composite asserts no medical proposition beyond its invented consult; the appeal turns on burden and completeness, not on the game adjudicating medicine.
- **S126 "In Her Own Words" (Complex).** Pattern: older youth's positions paraphrased in court reports, losing content in compression; placement-change notice practice; school continuity at a placement move (§§ 6311(g)(1)(E)(i)/6312(c)(5)(B)); ASFA date: entered care June 12, 2026; +15 months = **September 12, 2027** (machine-audited).
